## Hi, I'm Baojian Zhou (周宝健)

Assistant Professor, School of Data Science, Fudan University.

My research is in machine learning and mathematical optimization. Much of my work
concerns fast algorithms for large-scale graphs: locally evolving set processes,
personalized PageRank, online node labeling, and graph-structured sparse
optimization. I also work on the mathematical foundations of large language
models, including in-context learning and the training dynamics of diffusion
language models.
